# T-0005: Close w_M gap at D=5000 with Anderson acceleration + NaN detection

**Status**: DONE | **Size**: L
**Phase**: COMPLETE

## Context
After T-0004, phi_M matched C&D (1975) at all Dean numbers, but w_M had a 5% gap at D=5000 (427.7 vs 449.3) due to Fox corrections not converging. The outer Picard iteration at D=5000 has a very narrow basin of attraction — corrections beyond ~8% of full destabilize the iteration (NaN divergence or collapse to trivial zero solution).

## Acceptance Criteria
- [x] D=3500 grid (c): w_M within 0.1% of C&D 351.4
- [x] D=5000 grid (c): w_M within 0.1% of C&D 449.3
- [x] D<=2000 regression: no change in phi_M or w_M
- [x] No NaN or collapse during any run

## Approach

### What failed (v1–v12)
- Heavy under-relaxation (XI damping, Gauss-Seidel): made things worse
- Restart-on-collapse: accumulated corrections at stability limit, immediate re-NaN
- Wall-Omega convergence criterion: too tight with 2-cycle averaging
- Min-outer-iterations + NaN-detect-and-exit: best Picard result was w_M=434.0 (3.4% gap)

### What worked
1. **Anderson acceleration (Type-I, depth=4, beta=0.5)**: Stores last m iterates and residuals, solves constrained least-squares for optimal linear combination. Equivalent to GMRES on the fixed-point residual. Stabilizes the iteration across all 692 correction iterations at D=5000.

2. **ieee_is_finite NaN detection**: Added to SOR_PHI, SOR_W, SOR_OMEGA, and SMOOTH. Early exit on NaN prevents silent corruption (gfortran's MAX(a, NaN) returns a).

3. **Tight EPS_OUT for D=5000**: Changed from (1, 10, 100) to match EPS_CASES — Anderson handles convergence speed.

4. **Relaxed secondary convergence threshold**: 98% relative reduction (was 99%) because D=5000 E0 residual limit-cycles at 0.57, just above the 99% threshold.

- Anderson acceleration subroutine: ~130 lines in CONTAINS section
- SOLVE_SMALL helper: dense linear solver with partial pivoting for augmented system (n<=7)
- AA_ON enabled for D>=3500; depth=4, beta=0.5, regularization=1e-12
- History reset at each D-step and correction iteration
- Scaling weights (S_PHI, S_W, S_OMG) normalize fields before Gram matrix construction
- Coefficient safeguard: if max|alpha|>10, restart with newest iterate only
