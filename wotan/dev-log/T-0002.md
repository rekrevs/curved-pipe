**Status**: BLOCKED (after T-0001) | **Size**: S

## What

Make the Omega SOR "Jacobi boundary" fully consistent by copying all boundary frames to slice 2 before calling SOR_OMEGA, not just the wall.

We already added:
```fortran
OMEGA(NRP1, 1:NAP1, 2) = OMEGA(NRP1, 1:NAP1, 3)
```

Keep that. But to avoid any other silent boundary-staleness in slice 2 (and to make the omega solver fully well-posed in the Jacobi directions), also copy the other boundary frames right before calling SOR_OMEGA:

```fortran
OMEGA(1,    1:NAP1, 2) = OMEGA(1,    1:NAP1, 3)   ! r=0 boundary
OMEGA(1:NRP1,1,     2) = OMEGA(1:NRP1,1,     3)   ! alpha=0 boundary
OMEGA(1:NRP1,NAP1,  2) = OMEGA(1:NRP1,NAP1,  3)   ! alpha=pi boundary
```

This should not change D<=1000 results, but it removes another potential "boundary held hostage by slice-2 history" effect.
